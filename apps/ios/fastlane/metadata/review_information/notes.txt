Wain is a remote control for coding agents that run on the user's own computer. The app has no account and no content of its own: everything it shows comes from a machine running Wain (the Wain app for Mac, or the Wain server on Linux) that the user pairs with.

To review the app, pair it with our demo machine:
1. Open the app and tap Pair a machine.
2. Tap Enter an address, and enter: DEMO_ADDRESS
3. Enter the code: DEMO_CODE

The demo machine holds sample bots and tasks. You can open a task, reply to it, answer a question card, and start a new task. Its agents run in a sandbox with no access to anything else.
