This is Wain's privacy policy. It covers the Wain desktop app, the Wain apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, the `wain` command line and server, Wain accounts, and wainhq.com with these docs. Wain is run by Ismat Babirli; "we" and "us" here mean him. Write to [support@wainhq.com](mailto:support@wainhq.com) about anything on this page.

Last updated: 27 November 2026.

In short:

- Your prompts, code, transcripts and files stay on your own machines.
- Wain sends no analytics: no usage events, no error reports, no surveys.
- A [Wain account](#your-wain-account) holds your name, email address and the machines you sign in on.
- We sell nothing, show no ads and do not track you across other apps or sites.

## No analytics

Wain's builds send no usage events, error reports or surveys, from the app, the background server, the Linux server or the phone apps. The source code can send anonymous usage events when it is built with an analytics key; Wain's releases are built without one. If that ever changes, this page will say so first, and you will be able to turn it off by setting `WAIN_NO_ANALYTICS`.

When something breaks, the details stay on your machine, in `~/.wain`. An agent line Wain could not read, for one, is kept in `~/.wain/parse_failures.jsonl`.

## What never leaves your machine

- Your prompts, the agent's replies, and anything else in a transcript.
- Code, file names and file paths.
- Session titles, project names, branch names, issue titles.
- Server names and addresses, ports and links.
- Screens and recordings.

The one exception is a phone alert, once you turn alerts on: it carries a task's title and a line about it. See [Phone alerts](#phone-alerts).

Your agents talk to their own providers, as they do in a terminal; see [Other connections Wain makes](#other-connections-wain-makes).

## The website and these docs

wainhq.com and these docs run no analytics and set no tracking cookies. Signing in sets a session cookie, which keeps you signed in and nothing else.

Cloudflare carries every request to the site on its way to our server. It sees your IP address, as any network in the path does, and may set a cookie it needs to protect the site from attacks.

## Your Wain account

You sign in to the desktop app with a Wain account, in your browser on wainhq.com. The same account links your servers to an org.

You sign in with GitHub or Google. They tell us your name, email address and profile picture. We ask for nothing else, and get no access to your repositories or other data there. Email sign-in is not available yet.

Your account holds:

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your name and email address | To sign you in and show who you are. |
| Your profile picture, from GitHub or Google | Shown beside your name on wainhq.com. |
| A username, made from your email address | Yours to change in your account settings. |
| Your orgs and who is in them | So members of an org can share its servers. |
| The machines you sign in on or link, by the name each gives itself | So you can see and unlink them. On a Mac this is its computer name. |
| The IP address and browser of each sign-in on the site | Saved with that sign-in, to spot sign-ins that aren't yours. |

It is stored in a database on a server we run ourselves, at netcup in Austria.

### Change or delete your account

Change your name and username in your account settings on wainhq.com. To delete your account and everything it holds, email [support@wainhq.com](mailto:support@wainhq.com) from the address you sign in with. There is no delete button yet.

### Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what your account holds, to correct it, or to delete it, by writing to [support@wainhq.com](mailto:support@wainhq.com). We answer within 30 days. If you live in the EU or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

## Wain for iPhone, iPad and Android

The phone apps have no account and send no analytics. They connect to the machines you pair, directly or through Remote access, to wainhq.com for alerts, and to Wain's GitHub repository to see whether a paired machine runs an older Wain than the newest one.

The phone keeps each paired machine's name, address and access token: on iPhone in the Keychain, on Android encrypted with the Android Keystore. Your picks, such as which machines to show, stay on the phone too.

- **Camera.** Used only to scan a machine's pairing QR code. Nothing is recorded or sent.
- **Microphone.** Used only for dictation. On iPhone, Apple's speech models turn what you say into text on the phone, or the recording goes to your own Mac to be read there. On Android, the phone's speech recognition does it: on the phone where Android offers that, otherwise through the phone's speech service, usually Google's.
- **Photos.** On iPhone, Wain asks to add to your photos only when you save a picture.

### Phone alerts

Alerts are off until you allow them. Once you do, the phone sends wainhq.com its push token from Apple or Google. wainhq.com seals that token into a handle only it can read, and the phone gives the handle to the machines you pair. When a task finishes or an agent needs you, your machine sends the alert to wainhq.com with that handle, and wainhq.com passes it to Apple or Google, who deliver it.

An alert holds the task's title, a line about what happened and, for a question, its options. wainhq.com stores neither the token nor the alert, and logs no alert text. To cap how many alerts one phone gets, it keeps a count under a one-way hash of the token, for about a day.

On Android, alerts arrive through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Firebase gives the app an installation ID when it first opens, which Google uses to deliver alerts.

## Other connections Wain makes

Wain connects to the network for:

- **Your agents.** Each agent CLI talks to its own provider, as it does in a terminal. Wain also asks the session's own agent, on its cheapest model, to write each session's title from your first prompt. For fx, Wain reads Vercel's public model list to know each model's effort levels.
- **Updates.** Checking for and downloading new versions of the app and the `wain` CLI, from Wain's GitHub repository.
- **The changelog.** Reading what's new from wainhq.com.
- **Your Wain account.** Signing in through wainhq.com, and checking now and then that the sign-in still holds. The sign-in and this machine's link to your org are kept in `~/.wain/account.json` and `~/.wain/org-link.json`.
- **Downloads you trigger or need:** the Chromium framework for Wain's browser (from Spotify's CEF build server), dictation models (from Hugging Face).
- **GitHub and Linear,** when you use pull requests or issues, through `gh` or your Linear key.
- **OpenTunnel,** only while [Remote access](/reference/remote-access#remote-access) is on or a [public link](/reference/remote-access#share-a-dev-server) is live.

Dictation runs on your Mac. Your voice never leaves it.

## Services we use

- **Cloudflare** runs the domain's DNS and carries traffic to wainhq.com.
- **netcup**, in Austria, hosts wainhq.com, these docs and the database that holds Wain accounts, on a server we run.
- **Contabo**, in Germany, hosts the mail server for wainhq.com addresses, on a server we run. Mail you send to support@wainhq.com is kept there.
- **GitHub** and **Google** sign you in, when you choose them. GitHub also hosts Wain's source code and releases.
- **Apple** and **Google** deliver [phone alerts](#phone-alerts), through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging.

We sell no data, show no ads, and share nothing beyond these services.
