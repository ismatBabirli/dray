# Wain — logo brief

## The product
Wain is a desktop app (macOS first, also Linux and Windows) for software engineers who work with AI coding agents. It runs many agents side by side — Claude Code, Codex, Grok, pi, fx and OpenCode — each in its own session, and lets one person start, watch, steer and review all of them from one place. It also has a command-line tool, a headless server, and iPhone and Android companion apps. Free and open source; it runs on the AI subscriptions people already pay for.

Tagline on the site today: "Run every coding agent in one app."

## The name
"Wain" (one syllable, rhymes with "rain") is an old English word for a farm wagon: the thing that carries the load. "Charles's Wain" is the British name for the Big Dipper, the seven stars travellers navigated by; its two pointer stars lead to the North Star. Either meaning can inform the mark, but neither has to be drawn literally.

## Audience and feel
Professional developers. The app is quiet, precise and dense with information, like Linear, Raycast, Arc, Things or Zed. The brand should feel calm, confident, crafted and modern: a serious tool, not a toy, not a mascot, not "AI magic".

## Where the logo is used
- macOS app icon (and Windows/Linux), including a dark-mode variant
- Website favicon and header (wainhq.com), Open Graph image
- Sign-in screens: Google OAuth consent screen, GitHub OAuth app
- Later: iOS and Android app icons

## Visual context
- App UI: macOS "glass" window (vibrancy), near-black #0a0a0a in dark mode, near-white #f3f3f3 in light mode, one warm yellow accent (#e5c057 on dark, #bd8200 on light). Typeface: Geist and Geist Mono.
- These are the app's current colours, not a requirement. The logo may bring its own colour.

## Must
- One clear idea, with a distinctive silhouette still recognisable in solid black at 16×16 px.
- Works in one colour (black, white) and in full colour, on light and dark backgrounds.
- Geometric, precise, vector-clean: consistent stroke weights, real curves, optical balance.

## Must not
- Look like Dray's icon (Wain is a renamed fork of Dray): a black, blocky "D"-like shape on a white rounded square. Nothing derived from Dray's mark.
- Literal clip-art: wagon, cart, shopping cart, wheel, robot, brain, chat bubble, circuit, sparkle/AI-star.
- Gradients, glow, 3D, photorealism, textures, words in the mark.

## Already tried and rejected (don't repeat)
Literal wagon and cart; a spoked wagon wheel; a W with wheels for feet; Big Dipper drawn as dots; two overlapping Vs forming a W; a W with a star cut into it; a folded-ribbon W; three parallel lines forming a W; stacked tilted tiles forming a W; a lowercase w from two arcs; a crown or pointer with a star.

## Deliverables
- Master mark as SVG: full colour, plus black and white single-colour versions.
- Mark on a transparent background, 1024×1024 PNG (for the app icon's foreground layer), plus the background colour on its own.
- App icon 1024×1024 following Apple's macOS icon grid (rounded rectangle about 824×824 centred, transparent margin), light and dark variants.
- Favicon: SVG and 512×512 PNG, checked at 16 and 32 px; 180×180 Apple touch icon.
- Google OAuth logo: square 120×120 PNG, under 1 MB.
- GitHub OAuth app logo: square PNG, at least 200×200, under 1 MB.
- Open Graph image: 1200×630 PNG.
- Optional: wordmark "Wain" (or "wain") set to pair with the mark; Geist is the UI typeface.
